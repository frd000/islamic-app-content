Privacy policy – Deen Sathi

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy covers the Android app "Deen Sathi" (package name io.github.frd000.islamicapp).

In short

  • No ads. No analytics. No tracking.
  • Everything works without an account. Without an account, your data stays on your phone.
  • Your location never leaves your phone.
  • An account is optional. It only backs up your own app data, so it comes back on a new phone.
  • We never share or sell any data.

Who we are

The app is made by an individual developer in India (the "Data Fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). Developer and contact for privacy requests and complaints: Asif Rahman, 57asifrahman@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Location (approximate, or the city you choose): used on the phone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not use location in the background.
  • Your app data: settings, bookmarks, reading position, Khatam plan, Salah tracker, Qaza counts, Tasbih count and Zakat amounts.
  • If backup is switched on in your phone's Android settings, Android (Google) may include the app's settings in your phone backup. You control this in Android's settings.

Optional account ("Back up and sync")

Only if you choose to sign in with Google, and only after you agree on the consent screen in the app:

  • What is stored: your email address (from Google, to know it is you), the date you agreed and the app language, your bookmarks, last reading place and Khatam plan, Salah tracker and Qaza counts, and your app settings.
  • What is never stored: your location, your Zakat amounts, your name, contacts, age or gender.
  • Why: to back up your data and bring it back on another phone. Nothing else.
  • Where: Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Security rules let each user read and write only their own data. Your email is never shown to other users.
  • How long: until you delete your account.
  • Age: the account (and so posting in the forum) is only for people aged 18 or older, because Indian law asks for a parent's verifiable consent for anyone younger. Everything else in the app works without an account, at any age.

Community forum (Questions & Answers)

  • Anyone can read the forum. To ask or answer you must be signed in and accept the forum rules.
  • Public: your forum name (a nickname you choose) and what you post. Your email is never shown.
  • For moderation (not public): how many of your posts were approved, warnings, and whether you are banned. Reports and complaints are seen only by the moderators.
  • The quoted Quran and hadith texts are not stored with your post — only the reference (for example 2:255).

Grievance Officer (India IT Rules, 2021)

Asif Rahman — 57asifrahman@gmail.com. See the complaints page. Complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 7 days.

What we must keep by law

Under India's IT Rules, when an account is deleted we keep its sign-up email address for 180 days (only the administrator can see it), and posts removed by moderators are kept hidden for 180 days. After that they are deleted.

Deleting your account

  • In the app: Settings → Back up and sync → Delete account. This deletes your account, your backup and your forum posts from the server at once (a question that others answered stays, marked "question removed by its author", without its text). The sign-up email is kept for 180 days as the law requires. The data on your phone stays.
  • Without the app: see the account deletion page.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, withdraw your consent (delete the account), and make a complaint. Write to 57asifrahman@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Downloads from other websites

Some content is downloaded only when you ask for it: Quran recitation audio (Islamic Network CDN) and hadith collections (HadeethEnc.com). Like any download, these servers see your phone's internet (IP) address. We send them nothing else.

"Support this app"

Payments, if you choose to make one, are handled only by Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. The payment unlocks nothing; every feature is free for everyone.

Changes

If this policy changes in an important way, the app will ask for your consent again before your data is backed up.
